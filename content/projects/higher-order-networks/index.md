Many networks involve interactions among groups, rather than only pairs of entities.
During my PhD at Aarhus University, I developed spatial and dynamic models of these higher-order relationships with collaborators.
We established limit theorems for network statistics, including degree distributions, edge counts, and topological characteristics.
I implemented high-performance Monte Carlo algorithms in Python and C++ to investigate finite-sample behavior and assess theoretical predictions.
Fitting models and conducting hypothesis tests on scientific collaboration networks connected the mathematical results to empirical data.
The research is documented in my [thesis](/publications/phd_thesis/), work on [higher-order network topology](/publications/higher_order_adrcm/), and [dynamic hypergraphs](/publications/dynamic_hypergraphs/), alongside public simulation software and a collaboration dataset.
